# Project Design Context

> Single source of truth for the visual direction of this portfolio. Loaded by
> the `impeccable` design skills so future work proceeds without re-asking.

## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and prospective freelance clients evaluating
**Arkadiusz Grondys**, a full-stack developer (Angular / React / Node.js) in
Wrocław, PL. They scan fast — desktop in an office, sometimes mobile — and decide
within seconds whether he is credible and worth contacting. Job-to-be-done:
gauge skill, see real shipped work, find a way to reach out.

### Brand Personality
Precise, hand-built, confident. Three words: **structural, editorial,
unembellished**. Should feel like a printed design annual or a technical drawing,
never a SaaS landing page. Conveys craft and engineering rigor without shouting.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Brutalist Terminal — light theme.** Warm bone paper, warm near-black ink, a
single **oxblood** accent held to ~10% of the visual weight. Oversized
Expanded-Black grotesque display (Archivo driven to `wdth` 125 + heavy weight),
Hanken Grotesk for reading, Martian Mono for structural labels (coordinates,
indices, kickers used as architecture). Exposed grid + hairline rules, full-bleed
inverted ink bands, zero border-radius, zero drop-shadow, technical-drawing
coordinate motifs.

**Anti-references (explicitly avoid):** generic "AI template" dark mode,
purple/cyan glows, neon-on-dark, basic bento grids, centered hero with floating
abstract shapes, glassmorphism, gradient text, colored left-border accent stripes.

### Design Principles
1. **Structure as ornament** — mono labels, indices, coordinates and rules *are*
   the decoration. No extra flourish.
2. **One accent, used rarely** — oxblood for emphasis only; never a second accent.
3. **Type does the talking** — dramatic scale contrast (Expanded-Black masthead
   vs. small mono), left-aligned, ragged right, generous asymmetry.
4. **Hard edges, honest surfaces** — no radius, no shadow, no glass. Full borders
   and background tints instead.
5. **Motion is restrained and earned** — staggered ease-out reveals + one marquee.
   Everything gated behind `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Tokens (see `src/styles/global.css`)
OKLCH throughout, neutrals tinted toward the oxblood hue (~27°).
`--paper` bone · `--ink` warm near-black · `--oxblood` accent · hairline `--rule`.
Type: `font-display` (Archivo, `font-stretch:125%`), `font-sans` (Hanken Grotesk),
`font-mono` (Martian Mono).
